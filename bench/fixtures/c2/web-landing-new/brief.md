# Brief: Kelpie landing page

**Product.** Kelpie is a desktop app for people who run small tide-pool and
coastal monitoring projects (volunteer groups, school programmes, small
conservancies). It turns a folder of survey photos and a CSV of field notes
into a tidy species log and a monthly report.

**Audience.** Volunteer coordinators and biology teachers. Not technical. They
currently use shared spreadsheets and a lot of email.

**One-line promise.** "Turn a morning on the shore into a report by lunch."

**What it does (3 points).**
1. Reads your photos' timestamps and GPS tags and lines them up with the field
   notes CSV, so nobody retypes locations.
2. Suggests a species name for each photo, which you confirm or correct.
   Nothing is final until a person approves it.
3. Builds a monthly PDF report with a species table, counts per site and the
   photos you picked as highlights.

**Proof we can use (all true).**
- Used by 41 volunteer groups in a pilot, 2025 season.
- Average report time in the pilot went from "most of a weekend" to "under an
  hour" (self-reported by 19 coordinators).
- Works offline. Your photos stay on your computer.

**Pricing.** Free for groups of up to 5 volunteers. $9 per month for larger
groups, billed yearly. Free for schools.

**Call to action.** "Download for Mac or Windows" (primary). "See a sample
report" (secondary, links to sample-report.pdf).

**Tone.** Calm, a bit warm, plain words. Not corporate, not cartoonish. No
stock-photo language.

**Brand.**
- Colours: deep sea green #12433f, sand #f1e9d8, coral accent #e4573d, ink
  #1b2321.
- Typeface: whatever is safe without loading anything from the network.
- Logo: the word "Kelpie" in lowercase, no symbol yet.

**Must have.** Hero, the three points, proof, pricing, FAQ with 3 questions
(you write them from the facts above), footer with a contact address
hello@kelpie.example.
