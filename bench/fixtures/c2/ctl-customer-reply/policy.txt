Harbor Goods support policy (internal)

Late or lost orders
- If tracking has not moved for 5 business days, we treat the parcel as lost.
- For a lost parcel we offer the customer a choice: a replacement shipped
  express at our cost (2 business days from dispatch), or a full refund to the
  original payment method. Refunds are issued within 1 business day and the
  bank takes 3-5 business days to show them.
- We cannot guarantee a delivery date. Express means 2 business days from
  dispatch; dispatch happens within 1 business day of the customer's choice.
- If an order is for a gift with a stated deadline, offer the refund first,
  because the replacement may not arrive in time.

Tone
- Apologise once, plainly. No marketing language. Do not blame the carrier.
- Give the customer the choice and what happens next; do not ask them to
  contact us again.

Goodwill
- Customers with a previous problem order get a 15% code (use HARBOR15) on
  their next order, offered once.
