# Inventory software abandonment interview

Ask about one inventory software product the participant stopped using. Use the wording as written; the notes below are for the interviewer and are not additional questions.

1. **What was happening in your store when you first decided to try or adopt [product], and what were you hoping it would help you do?**
   - *Learn:* The trigger for looking at inventory software and the job the participant expected it to do.

2. **Thinking about the period when you used [product], how did it fit into the inventory tasks you handled during a typical day or week?**
   - *Learn:* How the software fit actual routines, including when it was only tried briefly versus used as part of ongoing work.

3. **Can you describe a specific time when using [product] affected how you handled inventory?**
   - *Learn:* Concrete benefits, friction, or problems experienced during a trial or regular use without assuming the product caused abandonment.

4. **How did the decision to stop using [product] unfold, from the last time you used it to when you stopped?**
   - *Learn:* The events, people, and considerations behind discontinuing it, including whether the participant left during a trial or after adopting it.

5. **Since stopping [product], how have you handled inventory, and what has that been like in practice?**
   - *Learn:* The participant’s current tools or workarounds and how their experience changed after leaving the product.
