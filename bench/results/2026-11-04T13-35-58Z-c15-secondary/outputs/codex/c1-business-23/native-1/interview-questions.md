# Small Retailer Inventory Software: Interview Questions

1. **What was happening in your business when you first decided to try or adopt the inventory software?**  
   *Helps identify the needs, events, or pressures that prompted the search and whether the person only tried the product or put it into regular use.*

2. **Please walk me through a recent workday when you used the software, from the first inventory task to the last.**  
   *Helps reveal how the product fit into actual routines, tools, and responsibilities.*

3. **Thinking back to the times you used it, what problems or obstacles, if any, came up?**  
   *Helps surface specific friction and consequences without assuming the experience was negative; for someone who only tried it, this can focus on the trial.*

4. **How did the decision to stop using it happen, and what were the key events leading up to that decision?**  
   *Helps distinguish a brief trial that was not continued from a decision to end ongoing use, and understand how the choice was made.*

5. **Since stopping, what have you used to handle inventory, and how has that worked in practice?**  
   *Helps understand the current replacement or workaround and what changed after leaving the software.*
