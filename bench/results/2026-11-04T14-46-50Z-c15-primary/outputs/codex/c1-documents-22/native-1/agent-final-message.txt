An **appendix** usually contains extra material that supports or expands on the report, such as a table of detailed survey results. An **annex** often contains supporting material that stands more on its own, such as a signed policy or a technical specification.

That distinction isn’t universal: organizations and style guides use the terms differently. Since you haven’t provided a required naming convention, either label can work.

**Simple rule:** Use *appendix* for material that extends the report; use *annex* for a self-contained supporting document. Pick one term and use it consistently.

For multiple sections, label them in order—**Appendix A, Appendix B** (or **Annex A, Annex B**)—give each a clear title, and refer to them by the same labels in the report text.

To recommend the best term, I’d want to know what the extra material is, whether it was created for this report or exists as a separate document, and whether your organization or audience has a preferred style guide.
