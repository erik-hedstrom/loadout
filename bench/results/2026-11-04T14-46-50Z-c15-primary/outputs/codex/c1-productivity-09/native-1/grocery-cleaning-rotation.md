# Grocery and Cleaning Rotation

| Week | Groceries | Cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Me | Partner |
| Week 2 | Partner | Me |
| Week 3 | Me | Partner |
| Week 4 | Partner | Me |

**Repeat and temporary swaps:** After Week 4, start again at Week 1 and keep alternating. If you temporarily swap a responsibility, note it and trade back in a later week so that, over time, each of you does the same number of grocery and cleaning shifts.
