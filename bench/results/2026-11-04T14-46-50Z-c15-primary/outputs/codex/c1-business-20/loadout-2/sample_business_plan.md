# Illustrative sample business plan

> This is a made-up example created because no plan or data was supplied. Replace it with your actual plan before using the conclusions.

## One-paragraph concept

An SMS-based reminder and easy-rescheduling service for independent physiotherapy clinics. The proposed buyer is the clinic owner or practice manager; patients receive reminders and can confirm or request a new slot. The intended outcome is fewer unused appointment slots without adding front-desk work.

## What would make the business fail?

The most dangerous chain is: clinics do not lose enough money to care, or cannot be reached / change their workflow; therefore they will not pay €79 monthly. Even if they buy, the product could fail if it cannot connect to scheduling reliably, creates more staff work, costs too much to support, or does not improve repeat usage and renewal.

## Evidence discipline

- **Evidence** means observed behavior or records: missed-visit rates, current spend, signed pilot agreements, payment, actual use, saved staff time, or renewal.
- **Belief** means a prediction: “owners will pay,” “SMS will fix it,” or “the clinic can be reached through partners.”
- Compliments, survey enthusiasm, and “I’d use that” statements are weak signals. Count a real commitment, workflow change, or payment much more heavily.
