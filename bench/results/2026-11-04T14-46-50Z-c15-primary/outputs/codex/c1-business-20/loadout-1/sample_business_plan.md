# Sample business plan (invented for this exercise)

This is a realistic placeholder, not information supplied by the founder. Replace it with your actual plan before treating any conclusion as specific to your business.

- **Customer:** Independent dental clinics with 2–10 dentists, initially in one metropolitan area; buyer is the practice manager or owner.
- **Problem:** Last-minute cancellations leave chair time unused. Staff call patients manually to fill openings, often too late or inconsistently.
- **Proposed solution:** A lightweight service that sends available appointment slots to a clinic's opted-in waitlist by SMS and lets patients claim them. Initial version could be concierge-assisted rather than integrated with practice software.
- **Pricing:** €199 per clinic per month, cancellable monthly; no setup fee.
- **Distribution:** Founder-led outreach to clinic managers, then referrals and partnerships with dental consultants/software vendors.
- **Costs:** Early manual service estimated at €40/month per clinic for messaging and support, plus founder time. Product build estimated at €25,000; ongoing software, support, and sales costs not yet validated.
- **Current evidence:** Founder has heard several clinic managers complain about cancellations and two said they would be interested in a solution. No paid pilots, observed workflow data, price tests, signed commitments, integration review, or retention data.

## Important boundary
Compliments and stated interest are weak evidence. Nothing in this sample establishes that clinics will pay €199, adopt the workflow, or keep using it. All numbers below are test thresholds chosen for this hypothetical example, not benchmarks.
