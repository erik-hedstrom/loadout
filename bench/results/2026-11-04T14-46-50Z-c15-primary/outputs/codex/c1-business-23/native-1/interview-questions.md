# Customer interview questions: inventory software abandonment

1. **What was happening in your business when you first decided to try or adopt [product], and what led you to give it a chance?**  
   *Learn:* The situation, needs, and events that prompted the initial trial or adoption.

2. **Thinking about the time you used [product], how did it fit into the way you handled inventory during a typical workday?**  
   *Learn:* How the software fit—or failed to fit—real routines and responsibilities.

3. **Can you describe a specific time when using [product] caused difficulty or extra work, if that happened?**  
   *Learn:* Concrete friction, problems, and their practical effects without assuming there were any.

4. **How did you come to stop using [product]—whether you stopped during a trial or after adopting it—and what happened around that decision?**  
   *Learn:* The path and circumstances of abandonment, distinguishing a trial that did not continue from ending established use.

5. **What have you used to manage inventory since stopping [product], and how has that worked in practice?**  
   *Learn:* The current alternative, including how it serves the work after abandonment.
