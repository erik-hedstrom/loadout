   *Helps identify the circumstances and needs that prompted adoption.*

2. **Thinking about a typical workday while you were using it, how did the software fit into the way you handled inventory tasks?**  
   *Helps reveal where it fit into or disrupted daily work.*

3. **Can you describe a time when the software caused a problem or fell short for you?**  
   *Helps surface specific difficulties that may have contributed to stopping.*

4. **How did the decision to stop using it come about?**  
   *Helps understand who was involved, what shaped the decision, and whether they had adopted it or only tried it.*

5. **What do you use now to manage inventory, and how did you come to use that approach?**  
   *Helps learn what replaced the software, if anything, and why.*
