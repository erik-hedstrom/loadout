# Family calendar: quick questionnaire

Hi! I’m thinking about making it easier for our family to coordinate plans. Could you answer a few questions? It should take about 3–4 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers, and it’s fine to skip anything you’re unsure about.

1. **How do you usually coordinate family plans?** *(Choose all that apply.)*
   - Texts or group chats
   - Phone calls or in-person conversations
   - Paper calendar or notes
   - A digital calendar or planning app
   - Someone else keeps track and tells people
   - Something else: __________

2. **What is the hardest part of keeping up with family plans?** *(Choose up to two.)*
   - Finding out when plans change
   - Knowing who is going
   - Remembering dates or times
   - Getting everyone’s schedules together
   - Finding details like the address or what to bring
   - I don’t have much trouble with this
   - Something else: __________

3. **How do you prefer to hear about a new or changed plan?** *(Choose one.)*
   - Text or chat message
   - Phone call
   - Email
   - A shared calendar I can check when I want
   - No preference
   - Something else: __________

4. **Which of these would be essential for you to use a family calendar?** *Essential means you’d really need it; choose all that apply, or “None.”*
   - See everyone’s plans in one place
   - Add or change a plan easily
   - See who is going
   - Get a reminder before a plan
   - See plan details, such as time and place
   - Have plans show up in a calendar I already use
   - None of these are essential for me
   - Something else: __________

5. **Which would be nice to have, but you could manage without?** *(Choose up to three.)*
   - Different colors for different people
   - Repeat a regular plan without entering it each time
   - RSVP with a simple “I’m going” or “Can’t make it”
   - Add notes, such as what to bring
   - Get a text when a plan changes
   - See plans in a list as well as on a calendar
   - None of these
   - Something else: __________

6. **How comfortable would you feel checking or updating a calendar on a phone or computer?** *(Choose one.)*
   - Comfortable doing both
   - Comfortable checking, but not updating
   - I’d want someone to show me first
   - I’d rather get plans another way
   - Not sure

7. **If we could get just one thing right, what should it be?** *(Choose one.)*
   - Everyone can see the latest plan
   - It’s easy to add or change plans
   - People get useful reminders
   - It’s clear who is going and where to be
   - It works with the calendars or devices people already use
   - Something else: __________
