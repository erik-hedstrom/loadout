# Help Shape Our Family Calendar

This short survey should take about 3–5 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers, and you don’t need to be especially comfortable with technology. Skip any question you’d rather not answer.

1. **How do you usually find out about family plans?**  
   Select all that apply:  
   ☐ Someone tells me in person or by phone  
   ☐ Text messages or a family group chat  
   ☐ Email  
   ☐ A shared paper calendar  
   ☐ A digital calendar or app  
   ☐ Other: __________

2. **How do you usually let relatives know about your own plans or changes?**  
   Select all that apply:  
   ☐ In person or by phone  
   ☐ Text message or group chat  
   ☐ Email  
   ☐ Add it to a shared calendar  
   ☐ I usually don’t share plans in advance  
   ☐ Other: __________

3. **How well does the current way of coordinating work for you?**  
   ☐ Very well  
   ☐ Mostly well  
   ☐ Neither well nor poorly  
   ☐ Not very well  
   ☐ Not at all well

4. **What, if anything, is frustrating about coordinating family plans now?**  
   Select up to two:  
   ☐ I don’t hear about plans in time  
   ☐ Details or changes get lost  
   ☐ It’s hard to tell who’s available  
   ☐ I get too many messages or reminders  
   ☐ I find digital tools hard to use  
   ☐ Nothing in particular  
   ☐ Other: __________

5. **Which of these would be essential for you in a family calendar?**  
   Select all that apply:  
   ☐ See upcoming plans in one place  
   ☐ Know who is attending  
   ☐ Get a reminder before an event  
   ☐ See changes or cancellations  
   ☐ Add or update plans easily  
   ☐ None of these are essential for me

6. **Which would be nice to have, but not essential?**  
   Select up to three:  
   ☐ Share event locations or directions  
   ☐ Add notes, such as what to bring  
   ☐ See birthdays and anniversaries  
   ☐ Keep track of rides or who is picking someone up  
   ☐ Choose how and when I get reminders  
   ☐ Other: __________

7. **If we could make just one improvement to family planning, what should we focus on first?**  
   Choose one:  
   ☐ Making plans easy to find  
   ☐ Making it easier to share availability  
   ☐ Sending timely reminders  
   ☐ Showing changes clearly  
   ☐ Keeping the calendar simple to use  
   ☐ Other: __________
