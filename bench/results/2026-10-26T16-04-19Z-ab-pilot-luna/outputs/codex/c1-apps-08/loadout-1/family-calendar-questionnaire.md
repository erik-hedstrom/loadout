# Help us plan a family calendar

I’m thinking about making a shared family calendar and would love your input before deciding what to build. There are no right answers. Please answer in whatever way is easiest—by typing, writing it down, or talking it through with me. This should take about 3–5 minutes. You can skip anything you’d rather not answer.

1. **How do you usually find out about family plans or let others know about your own?** (For example: calls, texts, a paper calendar, an app, or in person.)

2. **What works well about the way your family coordinates plans now?**

3. **What, if anything, is frustrating or easy to miss when coordinating plans?**

4. **How would you prefer to check or receive family plans?** (For example: a calendar on a phone or computer, a printed calendar, a text or reminder, or another way.)

5. **For each idea below, mark whether it would be essential for you, nice to have, or not useful.** “Essential” means you’d need it for a shared calendar to work for you.
   - See family members’ events and availability
   - Add or update an event, including its time and place
   - Get reminders about events
   - See changes or cancellations
   - Keep some events private or share them only with certain people
   - Use it without needing a smartphone or a new app
   - Other idea: ____________________

6. **Of the ideas you marked essential or nice to have, which one or two would help you most?**

7. **Is there anything else you’d want us to know about coordinating family plans?** (Optional)
