# Help shape our family calendar

Hi! I’m thinking about making a shared family calendar and would love to know what would be useful to you. There are no right or wrong answers, and you don’t need to be especially comfortable with apps or computers to answer. Skip anything you’d rather not answer. This should take about 3–5 minutes.

1. **How do you usually hear about or keep track of family plans?** Select all that apply.
   - Someone tells me in person or by phone
   - Text messages or a family group chat
   - Email
   - A paper calendar, note, or planner
   - A calendar app on my phone or computer
   - I usually check with someone when I need to know
   - Other: __________

2. **What kinds of family plans do you most need to keep track of?** Select up to three.
   - Meals or get-togethers
   - Birthdays and other important dates
   - Children’s activities, school events, or appointments
   - Visits, travel, or who is staying where
   - Who is picking up, driving, or helping
   - Other: __________

3. **What, if anything, is frustrating or difficult about coordinating family plans now?** Select any that fit, or write your own.
   - I don’t always hear about changes
   - I’m not sure who is coming or who is responsible for something
   - Details are spread across messages, calls, or calendars
   - I get too many reminders or messages
   - I have trouble using or checking the tools we use
   - Nothing in particular
   - Other: __________

4. **Which of these would be essential for you in a shared family calendar?** Choose up to three. “Essential” means you’d rely on it; it does not mean it must be included in the first version.
   - See plans and event details in one place
   - Add or change plans
   - Know who is invited or planning to come
   - Get reminders before plans
   - See who is driving, bringing something, or helping
   - Use it without installing an app (for example, by email or a web page)
   - Make it easy to read, with clear text and simple steps
   - None of these are essential for me
   - Other: __________

5. **Which of these would be nice to have, but not essential?** Choose up to three from the list above, or write your own. It’s fine to choose none.
   - My choices: __________
   - Something else: __________
   - Nothing else comes to mind

6. **If only one improvement could be worked on first, which would help you most?** Choose one.
   - Finding the latest plan and its details
   - Knowing about changes in time
   - Knowing who is coming or helping
   - Adding or updating plans easily
   - Getting fewer or more useful reminders
   - Making the calendar easier to read or access
   - Another improvement: __________
