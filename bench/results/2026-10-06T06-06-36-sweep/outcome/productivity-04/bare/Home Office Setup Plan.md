# Home Office Setup Plan

> Test-run sample: consolidated from three fictional notes created for this exercise. Measurements and shopping items are sample data.

## Goals
- Keep the desk clear enough for a notebook and laptop.
- Reuse the current desk before buying a replacement.

## Measurements
- Desk: 120 cm wide × 60 cm deep.
- Wall space: 180 cm wide.
- Outlet: 25 cm from the left corner.
- Measure the wall and the desk before ordering anything.
- Reference image: ![[Desk sketch.png]]

## Layout
- Put the charging tray on the left side, near the outlet.
- Leave 15 cm behind the desk for cable access.
- Sketch options in [[Room Layout|room sketch]].
- Related measurements: [[Home Office Setup Plan#Measurements|desk dimensions]].
- See [[Home Office Setup Plan#Layout|layout ideas]].

## Cable routing
- Route monitor and charging cables along the rear edge.
- Label both ends of each cable before bundling them.
- Keep the power strip reachable from the front of the desk.
- Check cable lengths against [[Home Office Setup Plan#Measurements|desk measurements]].
- Follow [[Home Office Setup Plan#Shopping|cable shopping list]].

## Shopping
- Buy six reusable cable ties.
- Consider an adhesive cable tray; check whether it works on the desk finish.
