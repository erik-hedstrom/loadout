# Home Office Setup

## Layout and ergonomics

- Place the desk perpendicular to the window to reduce screen glare.
- Keep the top of the monitor near eye level.
- Measure the alcove before ordering; the desk must be no wider than 120 cm.
- See [[Home Office Setup#Equipment and purchasing|equipment shortlist]].

## Equipment and purchasing

- [ ] Compare a 120 cm desk with the existing dining table.
- [ ] Choose a monitor arm after checking the monitor's VESA mount.
- [ ] Try a chair in person before buying.
- Layout constraints: [[Home Office Setup#Layout and ergonomics|desk layout]].
- Reuse the selection criteria in [[Buying Guide#Furniture|furniture criteria]].

## Daily habits

- Take a short movement break between focused work sessions.
- Clear the desk and put cables away at the end of the day.

## Weekly review

- On Friday, review comfort, glare, and any equipment still needed.
- Check [[Home Office Setup#Equipment and purchasing|equipment shortlist]] before making a purchase.
- Capture improvements in [[Weekly Review|weekly review]].
