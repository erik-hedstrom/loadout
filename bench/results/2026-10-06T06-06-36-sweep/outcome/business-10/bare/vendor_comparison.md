sample inputs, dated 7 October 2026. This recommendation applies only to those
samples. Sources: [Alder](sample_quote_alder.md), [Birch](sample_quote_birch.md),
[Cedar](sample_quote_cedar.md).

basis is **10 equivalent LP-200 printers, one site, EUR excluding VAT, over
three years**. This quantity and horizon are test assumptions, not user

## Trade-offs and recommendation

- **Price:** Cedar has the lowest equipment-only price (€3,800). Alder has the
  lowest known initial installed cost (€4,400). Birch has the lowest known
  three-year cost (€4,650), €110 below Alder and €250 below Cedar's subtotal
  before Cedar's unpriced extras. Alder's €250 initial saving against Birch is
  overtaken when its third €120 annual support charge becomes payable.
- **Lead time:** Birch offers the strongest delivery certainty. Cedar offers
  the earliest estimated dispatch, but cannot be called the fastest delivery
  without a transit commitment. Alder quotes a longer estimated delivery window.
- **Warranty:** Birch is strongest: three years with onsite parts, labor and
  travel. Alder offers two years but transfers repair freight to the customer.
  Cedar offers one year from dispatch with material coverage gaps.
- **Hidden costs and terms:** Birch is clearest on shipping, installation and
  recurring charges, and requires the smallest deposit. Its unspecified late
  fees, short validity and cancellation restriction still need attention.
  Alder has support charges and freight exposure; Cedar has the most unpriced
  items and demands full equipment/setup/year-one maintenance payment upfront.

**Recommend Birch, provisionally.** It has the lowest known three-year cost,
the strongest warranty and a firm delivery commitment. The €110 margin over
Alder is small, so unquoted charges could change the cost ranking. This relies
on ordering 10 equivalent printers at one site while prices remain valid,
using the mandatory services for three years, comparing on an ex-VAT basis,
and finding no material extra fees in the final terms. Actual failure rates
and downtime costs are unknown; a complete risk-adjusted TCO cannot be calculated.
If initial cash outlay dominates, Alder deserves consideration; if delivery
speed dominates, obtain Cedar's firm delivered date before ranking it.

## Questions to send the vendors

**Alder**

1. Can you commit to a delivered date, and what happens if it is missed?
2. What are both-way warranty freight costs, repair turnaround times and any
   charges for faults found outside warranty?
3. Does fleet support renew beyond three years, how is it cancelled, and what
   does it cover beyond the warranty?
4. Does the cancellation fee attract VAT, how is it deducted from the deposit,
   and when is the remaining deposit refunded?
5. What are consumables, printhead replacement and post-warranty repair prices?

**Birch**

1. What late-payment fees or interest apply?
2. What remedy applies to missed delivery, and how do you define business days?
3. What onsite response/repair time is promised, and are loan units available?
4. How and when is a pre-dispatch cancellation refund paid, and are there any
   other fees beyond the stated €581.25?
5. What are consumables, wear parts and support/repair prices after year three?

**Cedar**

1. What is the fixed delivered shipping price, its tax treatment, the applicable
   VAT rate, and the final VAT-inclusive order total?
2. What is the firm delivered date, including transit, and the remedy for delay?
3. Is physical installation needed, who performs it, and what does it cost?
4. Does warranty cover labor and outbound replacement freight? What are repair
   turnaround and freight costs, and can warranty start at delivery?
5. What do maintenance payments cover, how does renewal/cancellation work,
   and what liabilities remain if we exit during the three-year commitment?
6. What are cancellation/refund terms, late charges, the shipping payment
   deadline, and any other fees?
7. What are consumables, wear parts and post-warranty repair prices?

**All vendors:** Confirm that the quoted configuration and capacity meet the
same requirements, all required accessories/licenses are included, and no
additional compulsory charges apply. Request the full terms incorporated into
the quote before accepting it.
